# Return & Refund Policy

## Overview
We are committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with every purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, you may return eligible items within the specified return window.

## Return Window
- **Standard items**: 30 days from the date of delivery.
- **Electronics & appliances** (smartphones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches): 15 days from the date of delivery.
- **Earbuds, headphones, and audio accessories**: 15 days from the date of delivery (must be in original sealed packaging if unopened, or with all original accessories if opened).
- **Perishable goods (food, flowers)**: Non-returnable once delivered.
- **Digital products (software licenses, gift cards)**: Non-returnable and non-refundable.

## Eligibility Conditions
To be eligible for a return, the item must:
1. Be in its original, unused condition.
2. Include all original packaging, tags, and accessories.
3. Be accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase (order number is sufficient).

Items that are damaged due to customer misuse, normal wear and tear, or unauthorized modifications are **not eligible** for return.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Log in to your account and navigate to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click **Request Return** and select a reason.
4. Choose your preferred option: **Pickup from address** or **Self-drop at partner location**.
5. For pickup: Our logistics partner will visit your address within **2-3 business days** to collect the item.
6. For self-drop: Drop the item at the nearest Delhivery/DTDC/BlueDart outlet.

Alternatively, you can contact our support team via chat or email to initiate a return.

## Return Pickup (India)
- **Free pickup** is available for most PIN codes across India.
- Pickup is scheduled within **2-3 business days** of return approval.
- You will receive an SMS/email notification with the pickup date and time slot.
- Please keep the item packed and ready for the pickup agent.
- For remote PIN codes where pickup is not available, we provide a prepaid shipping label for self-drop.

## Refund Processing

### Refund Timelines by Payment Method

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline (from return approval) |
|----------------|---------------------------------------|
| UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) | **2-3 business days** |
| Credit Card | **5-7 business days** |
| Debit Card | **5-7 business days** |
| Net Banking | **5-7 business days** |
| Digital Wallet (Paytm, Amazon Pay) | **1-2 business days** |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | **5-7 business days** (refund via NEFT to bank account) |

### Refund Method
- **Prepaid orders**: Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used during purchase.
- **COD orders**: Refunds are transferred to your bank account via NEFT. You will be asked to provide your bank account details (account number, IFSC code).
- **Store Credit**: You may choose to receive the refund as store credit (instant, valid for 12 months).

### Partial Refund
A partial refund may be issued if:
- The returned item is not in its original condition.
- Accessories or packaging are missing.
- The item shows signs of use beyond what is necessary to test it.

## Exchange Policy
- **Direct exchange** is available for select products (size/color variants).
- To exchange, initiate a return and select "Exchange" as the reason.
- The replacement will be shipped after we receive and verify the returned item.
- If the exchange item is of a higher value, you will be asked to pay the difference.
- If the exchange item is of a lower value, the difference will be refunded.

## Warranty Claims
For products under manufacturer warranty:
1. Contact our support team with your **order number** and a description of the issue.
2. Our team will verify the warranty status and guide you through the process.
3. Warranty repairs are typically completed within **10-15 business days**.
4. If the product cannot be repaired, a replacement or refund will be offered.

### Warranty Coverage
- **Smartphones**: 1-year manufacturer warranty
- **Laptops**: 1-year manufacturer warranty (extendable)
- **Tablets**: 1-year manufacturer warranty
- **Smartwatches**: 1-year manufacturer warranty
- **Audio products**: 1-year manufacturer warranty
- **Home appliances**: 1-2 years manufacturer warranty (varies by product)

### Extended Warranty
Extended warranty plans are available for select products at the time of purchase or within 15 days of delivery. Plans range from 1 to 3 additional years.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Items damaged due to misuse or negligence
- Items with removed or tampered serial numbers
- Gift cards and digital vouchers
- Items marked as "Non-Returnable" on the product page
- Items returned after the return window has expired

## Cancellation Policy
- **Before shipping**: Full refund, no questions asked.
- **After shipping but before delivery**: You may refuse delivery. Full refund will be processed after the item is returned to our warehouse.
- **After delivery**: Follow the return process described above.
