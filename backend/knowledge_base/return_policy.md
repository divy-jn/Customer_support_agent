# Return & Refund Policy

## Overview
We are committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with every purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, you may return eligible items within the specified return window.

## Return Window
- **Standard items**: 30 days from the date of delivery.
- **Electronics & appliances**: 15 days from the date of delivery.
- **Perishable goods (food, flowers)**: Non-returnable once delivered.
- **Digital products (software licenses, gift cards)**: Non-returnable and non-refundable.

## Eligibility Conditions
To be eligible for a return, the item must:
1. Be in its original, unused condition.
2. Include all original packaging, tags, and accessories.
3. Be accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase (order number is sufficient).

Items that are damaged due to customer misuse, normal wear and tear, or unauthorized modifications are **not eligible** for return.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Log in to your account and navigate to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click **Request Return** and select a reason.
4. Print the prepaid shipping label (provided for most domestic returns).
5. Pack the item securely and drop it off at an authorized shipping partner location.

Alternatively, you can contact our support team via chat or email to initiate a return.

## Refund Processing
- **Refund method**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method used during purchase.
- **Processing time**: Refunds are processed within **5–7 business days** after we receive and inspect the returned item.
- **Partial refunds**: Items returned in a damaged or incomplete state may be subject to a partial refund or restocking fee of up to 15%.

## Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different item, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

## Refund Status
You can track your refund status by:
1. Checking **My Orders > Return Status** in your account.
2. Contacting our support team with your order number.

## Exceptions
- **Customized or personalized items**: Non-returnable unless defective.
- **Clearance / Final Sale items**: Non-returnable and non-refundable.
- **Subscription services**: Subject to the cancellation policy, not the return policy.
