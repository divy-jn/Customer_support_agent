# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Orders

### How do I place an order?
Browse our catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or create an account for faster future purchases. All prices are displayed in INR (₹).

### Can I modify my order after placing it?
Yes, you can modify your order within **1 hour** of placing it, provided it has not yet been processed. Go to **My Orders** and click **Edit Order**. After processing begins, modifications are no longer possible.

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Go to **My Orders**, select the order, and click **Cancel Order**. If the order has already shipped, you will need to initiate a return after delivery.

### How do I track my order?
Go to **My Orders** in your account. Each order has a tracking number that links to the carrier's tracking page. You will also receive tracking updates via email and SMS. Our delivery partners include Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, Ekart, and XpressBees.

### What are the delivery timelines?
- **Metro cities** (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad): **2-4 business days**
- **Tier-2 cities** (Jaipur, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Kochi, Indore, etc.): **4-6 business days**
- **Other areas**: **7-10 business days**
- **Remote/hilly areas**: **10-15 business days**

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary during sale periods, festivals (Diwali, Republic Day Sale), or adverse weather conditions.

## Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
- **UPI**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, Amazon Pay UPI
- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay
- **Net Banking**: All major Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, etc.)
- **Wallets**: Paytm Wallet, PhonePe Wallet, Amazon Pay Balance
- **EMI**: No-cost EMI available on select credit cards (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak)
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available for orders up to ₹50,000 in serviceable PIN codes
- **Buy Now, Pay Later**: Available via select partners

### Is UPI safe for payments?
Yes. UPI payments are processed through the NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) infrastructure and are fully encrypted. You will receive a UPI transaction reference number for every successful payment.

### My UPI payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed. What do I do?
If your UPI payment was deducted but the order shows as "Payment Failed" or "Pending":
1. **Wait 30 minutes** — sometimes there is a delay in payment confirmation from the bank.
2. If the order is still not confirmed after 30 minutes, contact our support team with your **UPI transaction reference number**.
3. If the payment was indeed debited and the order was not created, we will initiate a **full refund within 3-5 business days** to your bank account.

### Why was my payment declined?
Common reasons include:
- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect card details or UPI PIN
- Bank's fraud protection triggered
- Expired card
- Daily transaction limit exceeded
- UPI app not updated to latest version

Please verify your details or contact your bank. You can also try a different payment method.

### Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?
COD is available for most PIN codes across India for orders up to **₹50,000**. COD is not available for:
- Orders above ₹50,000
- Select remote PIN codes
- Certain high-value electronics (as specified on the product page)

The delivery person accepts **cash only** at the time of delivery. Card payment at delivery is not supported.

### Do you offer No-Cost EMI?
Yes, no-cost EMI is available on select products with the following credit cards:
- **HDFC Bank**: 3, 6, 9, 12 months
- **ICICI Bank**: 3, 6, 9 months
- **SBI Card**: 3, 6 months
- **Axis Bank**: 3, 6, 9 months
- **Kotak Mahindra Bank**: 3, 6 months

EMI options are displayed at checkout. Minimum order value for EMI is ₹3,000.

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes. All transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL encryption. We are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant and do not store your full credit card details on our servers.

## GST & Invoicing

### Will I receive a GST invoice?
Yes, a GST-compliant invoice is automatically generated for every order and sent via email. The invoice includes:
- Seller GSTIN
- HSN/SAC codes
- CGST, SGST, or IGST breakup
- Invoice number and date

### Can I get a GST invoice for business purchases?
Yes. Add your GSTIN during checkout or contact support with your **order number** and **GSTIN** to get a business GST invoice. The GST invoice will be updated with your business details.

### What is the GST rate on electronics?
Most electronics (smartphones, laptops, tablets, headphones, smartwatches) are charged at **18% GST**. Some accessories may be charged at **12% GST**. The exact GST rate is displayed on each product page and on the invoice.

## Account

### How do I create an account?
Click **Sign Up** on the top right corner of the page. You can register with your email address or mobile number. We will send an OTP to verify your identity.

### I'm not receiving the OTP. What should I do?
If you are not receiving the OTP:
1. Check your **spam/junk folder** (for email OTP).
2. Ensure you have **network connectivity** (for SMS OTP).
3. Wait **60 seconds** before requesting a new OTP.
4. Try the **alternate method** (email if SMS fails, or vice versa).
5. If the issue persists, contact our support team.

### How do I reset my password?
Click **Forgot Password** on the login page. Enter your registered email address and we will send a password reset link. The link expires in **24 hours**.

### How do I delete my account?
Contact our support team to request account deletion. Please note that account deletion is permanent and all order history, saved addresses, and preferences will be lost. Per RBI guidelines, we retain financial transaction records for **7 years** even after account deletion.

## Shipping

### Do you offer free shipping?
Yes, free shipping is available on orders above **₹499** to most serviceable PIN codes. For orders below ₹499, a flat shipping charge of **₹49** applies.

### Can I get same-day or next-day delivery?
Express delivery is available in select metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune) for an additional fee:
- **Same-day delivery**: ₹149 (order before 12 PM)
- **Next-day delivery**: ₹99

Express delivery is subject to product availability and PIN code serviceability.
