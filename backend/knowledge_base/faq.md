# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Orders

### How do I place an order?
Browse our catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or create an account for faster future purchases.

### Can I modify my order after placing it?
Yes, you can modify your order within **1 hour** of placing it, provided it has not yet been processed. Go to **My Orders** and click **Edit Order**. After processing begins, modifications are no longer possible.

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Go to **My Orders**, select the order, and click **Cancel Order**. If the order has already shipped, you will need to initiate a return after delivery.

### How do I track my order?
Go to **My Orders** in your account. Each order has a tracking number that links to the carrier's tracking page. You will also receive tracking updates via email.

## Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Net Banking
- UPI (for customers in India)
- Buy Now, Pay Later (Klarna, Afterpay)

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes. All transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL encryption. We are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant and do not store your full credit card details on our servers.

### Why was my payment declined?
Common reasons include:
- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect card details
- Bank's fraud protection triggered
- Expired card

Please verify your details or contact your bank. You can also try a different payment method.

## Account

### How do I create an account?
Click **Sign Up** on our website, enter your email and create a password. You can also sign up using your Google or Apple account.

### How do I reset my password?
Click **Forgot Password** on the login page. Enter your registered email, and we will send you a password reset link valid for 24 hours.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes. Go to **Account Settings > Delete Account**. This action is permanent and will remove all your data, order history, and saved preferences. Allow up to 30 days for complete data deletion per our privacy policy.

## Subscriptions

### How do I cancel a subscription?
Go to **Account Settings > Subscriptions** and click **Cancel**. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. No refunds are issued for partial billing periods.

### Can I change my subscription plan?
Yes. Go to **Account Settings > Subscriptions > Change Plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Technical Support

### My product is not turning on. What should I do?
1. Ensure the device is fully charged (charge for at least 30 minutes).
2. Try a different charger or cable.
3. Perform a hard reset (consult the product manual for instructions).
4. If the issue persists, contact our support team for warranty assistance.

### My app is crashing. What should I do?
1. Ensure your app is updated to the latest version.
2. Clear the app cache (**Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Clear Cache**).
3. Restart your device.
4. Uninstall and reinstall the app.
5. If the issue persists, contact our support team with your device model and OS version.

### I'm having connectivity issues with my smart home device.
1. Ensure your Wi-Fi network is 2.4 GHz (most smart home devices do not support 5 GHz).
2. Restart your router and the device.
3. Remove the device from the app and re-pair it.
4. Ensure the device firmware is up to date.
5. Contact support if the issue persists.
