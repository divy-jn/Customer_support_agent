# Account Management Guide

## Creating an Account
1. Visit our website and click **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and create a password (minimum 8 characters, must include a number and a special character).
3. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
4. Your account is now active and ready to use.

You can also sign up using:
- **Google Account**: Click "Continue with Google"
- **Apple ID**: Click "Continue with Apple"

## Account Types

| Type | Features | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **Basic** | Standard shopping, order tracking, customer support access | Free |
| **Premium** | Free express shipping, early access to sales, priority support, extended return window (45 days) | $9.99/month or $99/year |
| **Business** | Bulk pricing, dedicated account manager, invoice billing, API access | Custom pricing |

## Editing Your Account
Go to **Account Settings** to update:
- **Personal Info**: Name, phone number, date of birth
- **Email**: Changing email requires re-verification
- **Password**: Must re-enter current password to set a new one
- **Shipping Addresses**: Add, edit, or remove saved addresses (up to 5)
- **Payment Methods**: Add, edit, or remove saved cards (up to 3)
- **Communication Preferences**: Email notifications, SMS alerts, marketing preferences

## Switching Between Accounts
If you have both a personal and business account:
1. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner.
2. Click **Switch Account**.
3. Select the account you want to switch to.
4. You may need to re-authenticate.

## Deleting Your Account
1. Go to **Account Settings > Delete Account**.
2. Enter your password to confirm.
3. Select a reason for deletion (optional).
4. Click **Confirm Delete**.

**Important**:
- Account deletion is **permanent** and cannot be undone.
- All order history, saved addresses, and preferences will be removed.
- Active subscriptions will be cancelled.
- Pending orders will still be fulfilled.
- Complete data erasure takes up to **30 days** per our privacy policy.

## Password Recovery
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link (valid for 24 hours).
4. Click the link and set a new password.

If you do not receive the email:
- Check your spam/junk folder.
- Ensure you are using the correct email address.
- Contact support if the issue persists.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
For enhanced security, enable 2FA:
1. Go to **Account Settings > Security**.
2. Click **Enable 2FA**.
3. Choose your method: **Authenticator App** (recommended) or **SMS**.
4. Follow the setup instructions.

Once enabled, you will need to enter a verification code each time you log in from a new device.
