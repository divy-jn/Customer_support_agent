# Troubleshooting Guide

## Smartphones & Tablets

### Device Not Turning On
1. **Charge the device** for at least 30 minutes using the original charger and cable.
2. **Try a different outlet** or USB port.
3. **Perform a hard reset**: Press and hold the Power button + Volume Down button for 15 seconds.
4. **Check for physical damage**: Look for cracks, water damage indicators, or swollen battery signs.
5. If none of the above works, the device may have a hardware issue. Contact support for a warranty claim.

### Device Overheating
1. Remove the phone case to improve heat dissipation.
2. Close all background apps.
3. Avoid using the device while charging.
4. Turn off features you are not using (GPS, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi).
5. If the device consistently overheats above 45°C, contact support.

### Battery Draining Fast
1. Check **Settings > Battery** to identify apps consuming excessive power.
2. Reduce screen brightness or enable auto-brightness.
3. Disable unnecessary background app refresh.
4. Turn off location services for apps that do not need it.
5. If the battery health is below 80%, consider a battery replacement (covered under warranty if within the warranty period).

### Screen Unresponsive / Touch Issues
1. Clean the screen with a soft, lint-free cloth.
2. Remove any screen protector and test.
3. Restart the device.
4. Boot into Safe Mode to check if a third-party app is causing the issue.
5. Perform a factory reset as a last resort (back up your data first).

## Laptops & Computers

### Laptop Not Booting
1. Ensure the power adapter is connected and the LED indicator is on.
2. Remove any external devices (USB drives, monitors).
3. Perform a hard reset: Hold the power button for 15 seconds, release, then press it again.
4. If you see a blinking cursor or error message, note it down and contact support.

### Slow Performance
1. Restart the laptop.
2. Check available storage (**Settings > Storage**). Keep at least 15% of your drive free.
3. Uninstall unused applications.
4. Run a malware scan.
5. Check for OS and driver updates.
6. If the laptop has less than 8GB RAM, consider a RAM upgrade (where possible).

### Wi-Fi Connectivity Issues
1. Restart your router and the laptop.
2. Forget the Wi-Fi network and reconnect.
3. Run the built-in network troubleshooter (**Settings > Network > Troubleshoot**).
4. Update the Wi-Fi driver via Device Manager.
5. Try connecting to a different network to isolate the issue.

## Smart Home Devices

### Device Not Connecting to Wi-Fi
1. Ensure your Wi-Fi network is **2.4 GHz** (most smart devices do not support 5 GHz).
2. Place the device within 30 feet of the router during setup.
3. Restart the router and the device.
4. Reset the device to factory settings and re-pair it via the app.
5. Check if your router has a MAC address filter enabled (disable it temporarily).

### Voice Assistant Not Responding
1. Ensure the microphone is not muted (check the physical mute button).
2. Check that the device is connected to the internet.
3. Restart the device.
4. Re-train the wake word in the app settings.
5. If the issue persists, perform a factory reset.

## Audio Equipment

### No Sound from Headphones/Speakers
1. Ensure the device is charged and powered on.
2. Check the Bluetooth connection (unpair and re-pair).
3. Try connecting via a wired cable (if supported).
4. Increase the volume on both the audio device and the connected device.
5. Reset the audio device to factory settings.

### Audio Quality Issues (Static, Distortion)
1. Move closer to the connected device (Bluetooth range is typically 30 feet).
2. Remove obstructions between the devices.
3. Check if the issue occurs with different audio sources.
4. Update the firmware of the audio device via the companion app.

## Gaming

### Console Not Displaying on TV
1. Ensure the HDMI cable is securely connected to both the console and the TV.
2. Try a different HDMI port on the TV.
3. Try a different HDMI cable.
4. Set the TV input to the correct HDMI source.
5. Restart both the console and the TV.

### Controller Not Connecting
1. Ensure the controller is charged.
2. Try connecting via USB cable first, then switch to wireless.
3. Reset the controller (small pinhole button on the back, press with a paperclip for 5 seconds).
4. Re-pair the controller via the console settings.

## General Tips
- **Always keep your devices updated** with the latest firmware and software.
- **Use original accessories** (chargers, cables) to avoid compatibility and safety issues.
- **Back up your data regularly** to avoid data loss during troubleshooting.
- **Check our FAQ page** before contacting support — your issue may already have a documented solution.
