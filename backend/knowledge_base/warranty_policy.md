# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty Coverage
All products sold through our store come with a manufacturer's warranty. The warranty period varies by product category:

| Product Category | Warranty Period | Coverage |
|-----------------|----------------|----------|
| Smartphones & Tablets | 1 year | Hardware defects, battery issues |
| Laptops & Computers | 2 years | Hardware defects, screen issues |
| Smart Home Devices | 1 year | Hardware defects, connectivity issues |
| Audio Equipment | 1 year | Hardware defects |
| Gaming Consoles & Controllers | 1 year | Hardware defects |
| Cameras & Photography | 1 year | Hardware defects, lens issues |
| Home Appliances | 2 years | Motor, compressor, and hardware defects |
| Wearables & Smartwatches | 1 year | Hardware defects, sensor issues |
| Software | 90 days | Installation and activation issues only |

## What Is Covered
- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
- Hardware malfunctions under normal use conditions.
- Battery defects (if battery retains less than 80% capacity within the warranty period).

## What Is NOT Covered
- Damage caused by accidents, misuse, or unauthorized modifications.
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, dents from regular use).
- Water damage (unless the product is rated as water-resistant).
- Software issues, viruses, or data loss.
- Damage caused by use of non-approved accessories or chargers.
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers.

## How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Locate your **order number** and product serial number.
2. Contact our support team via chat, email, or phone.
3. Describe the issue and provide photos or videos if applicable.
4. Our team will evaluate the claim and provide a resolution within **3–5 business days**.

## Warranty Resolution Options
Depending on the issue, we may offer:
- **Repair**: Send the product to our service center for free repair.
- **Replacement**: Receive a new or refurbished unit of the same model.
- **Store Credit**: If the product is no longer available, receive store credit for the original purchase price.

## Extended Warranty
We offer extended warranty plans at the time of purchase:
- **1 additional year**: 10% of the product price.
- **2 additional years**: 18% of the product price.
- Extended warranty can be purchased within 30 days of the original purchase date.
